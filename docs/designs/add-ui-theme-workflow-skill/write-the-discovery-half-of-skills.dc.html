STEP 7 · HOW THE SKILL FINDS A PROJECT'S STYLE
Discovery, then the mapping table
1 · Stylesheet sources
All .css under the project; skip dependency and build output, skip minified or generated files.
2 · Token match
:root, :host, html custom properties, matched by role words.
3 · Frequency ranking
Most frequent colour, border-radius and font-family literals fill unbound roles.
4 · Dark-scheme detection
Media query, .dark or [data-theme="dark"] — recorded verbatim, never invented.
5 · Capped interview
At most six questions — primary, text, surface, border, radius, font. Every other role keeps its shipped fallback.
